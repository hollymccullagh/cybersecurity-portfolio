## Overview
 
Concepts explored throughout this book supported a broader understanding of networking, defensive security thinking, protocol analysis, and how modern systems communicate beneath high-level abstractions.

### My Review

Rather than promoting extreme paranoia, Zalewski acknowledges the fine line between maintaining reasonable security awareness and still being able to practically enjoy and use modern systems, networks, and internet-connected technologies. After reading this book, I am still not entirely sure which side of that fence I sit on — although, I now have a much deeper appreciation for how surprisingly important random number generation really is. As well as a newly acquired tendency to question what information I can derive from each blinkenlight I encounter. 

I am a big fan of Michal Zalewski's work and although published a couple of decades ago, this book still holds remarkable relevance in understanding modern networking, system behaviour, protocol design, and security thinking. Still very much a relevant read to this day.
