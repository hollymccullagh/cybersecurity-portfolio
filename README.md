# cybersecurity-portfolio
This space documents my ongoing development in cybersecurity. 

Through building things, securing them, breaking them, defending them, and fixing them, I can understand why things behave the way they do, and learn how to best protect them. 

This work reflects a genuine enjoyment of learning, and desire to improve my skills.

## Projects
My projects are organised into five domains.

Rather than grouping projects by specific technologies, I chose to organise them by where they fit within a typical cybersecurity workflow.

This structure reflects how I learn best—understanding how systems are built, secured, tested, defended, and continuously improved.

- **[Build](./projects/build/)** - Create and deploy, systems.

- **[Secure](./projects/secure/)** - Apply security controls to establish trust and harden systems.

- **[Break](./projects/break/)** - Test and challenge those controls through security assessment, enumeration, and exploitation.

- **[Defend](./projects/defend/)** - Monitor, detect, and respond to security threats.

- **[Learn](./projects/learn/)** - Improve knowledge through research, documentation, and information management.
